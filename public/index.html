FAQ

Questions people ask

How do I book a photographer in Hyderabad?

Pick a shoot type and area, compare verified creators and packages, then send a request. The creator accepts, you pay, and the booking is confirmed.

When is my booking confirmed?

Only after the creator accepts and your payment is verified. A request or a started payment alone does not confirm a booking.

What if the photographer cancels?

You are told straight away and shown replacement creators where available. You can accept one or take a full refund.

What is included in the price?

The price shown is all-in. Each package lists shoot hours, number of edited deliverables and the delivery deadline.

What if my photos arrive late or are not as agreed?

Report it from your booking. Support reviews the agreed scope and delivery record and decides on a fix or a refund. Read the Refund and Cancellation Policy for the details.

How are creators verified?

Creators submit ID and portfolio, and are reviewed manually before they are listed. Verification confirms identity, not artistic quality.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

Payments go through Razorpay. We never see or store your card or UPI details. You pay only after the creator accepts.
