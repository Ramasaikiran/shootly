How to cancel

Open your booking and choose Cancel booking. You can cancel while the booking is Requested, Accepted or Confirmed. Once the shoot has started, use Report an issue after delivery instead.

If a creator cancels

We tell you straight away and show replacement creators who are free at that time and charge the same or less. You can accept one or take a full refund. We never promise a replacement unless one is available.

Late or missing files

Each booking shows a delivery date. After that date, a Report missing files button appears on your booking. We contact the creator. If the files still do not arrive, you get a full refund.

Files that are not as agreed

Report an issue within 7 days of delivery and describe the problem. Support compares the delivery with the package you booked (hours, number of edited items, format) and the creator’s portfolio, then decides: ask the creator to fix it, a partial refund, or a full refund. We aim to decide within [X working days].

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. We start approved refunds within [2 working days]. Your bank or card issuer then usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show it.

Not refundable

  • Changes of mind after the files were delivered as agreed.
  • Differences of taste when the files match the package.
  • Delays or problems caused by the customer, such as wrong address or an unavailable venue.
