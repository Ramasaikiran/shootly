Who we are

[Company legal name], [Registered address, Hyderabad, Telangana], runs Shootly and decides how your personal data is used. This policy follows the principles of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email, a hashed password (we never keep the password itself), your role, and the time you accepted our policies.
  • Creators: area, shoot types, packages, portfolio and, when you submit them for verification, identity documents.
  • Bookings: date, time slot, shoot type, status and the history of changes.
  • Payments: order and payment IDs, amount and status. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay and never reach us.
  • Support: messages you send us.
  • Technical: your IP address, used for security and rate limiting, and one session cookie.

Why we use it

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Run bookings, payments, refunds and delivery.
  • Verify creators and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Answer support requests and resolve disputes.
  • Meet legal and tax duties.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads.

Who sees it

  • The other side of a booking. Customers see a creator’s name, area, packages and reviews. Creators see the customer’s name and the booking details.
  • Razorpay, to process payments and refunds.
  • Our hosting provider ([name]), to run the service.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.
