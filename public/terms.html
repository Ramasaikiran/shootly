How a booking works

  • The creator accepts or declines. Nothing is charged at this stage.
  • If the creator accepts, you pay through Razorpay.
  • The booking is confirmed only after the creator has accepted and your payment is verified. A request, or a payment you started but did not finish, does not confirm a booking.

The package scope, price and delivery deadline are fixed when you send the request. A creator changing a package later does not change your booking.
